---

🔥 Problem-Solving Rule

Har topic par:

1. Concept revise — 30–60 min

↓

2. 2–3 basic problems

↓

3. 5–10 Easy/Medium problems independently

↓

4. Jo nahi hua → solution/tutorial

↓

5. 2–3 days later → bina dekhe re-solve

↓

6. Pattern note karo

Bas tutorial dekhkar code kar dena second round ka goal nahi hai.

Final target

Fresher ke liye main roughly 180–250 well-understood problems ka target rakhunga, rather than blindly 400–500 questions.

Aur jab tum kisi Easy/Medium problem ko dekhkar 15–20 min mein approach identify karne lago, aur common patterns repeatedly recognize hone lagen, tab samjho DSA genuinely strong ho raha hai.

Tum ab beginner stage mein nahi ho; tumhare liye ab main focus "complete DSA" nahi, "interview-level problem solving" hona chahiye.
